Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Spendly ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our personal expense tracking application ("Service"). By using Spendly, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Data We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and password when you create an account, along with the expense data you enter — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We may also collect basic technical information, such as browser type and access times, to help operate the Service.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to provide core functionality — logging expenses, generating category breakdowns and summaries, and maintaining your account. We may also use aggregated, non-identifying data to understand usage patterns and improve the Service. We do not use your expense data for advertising purposes.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in our database and protected using reasonable administrative and technical safeguards. We retain your data for as long as your account remains active, or as needed to provide the Service. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell your personal data to third parties. We may rely on third-party infrastructure providers (such as hosting or email delivery services) to operate the Service, and these providers only process data on our behalf and under appropriate confidentiality obligations.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us through the Service.
